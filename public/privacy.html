UAV Companion privacy policy

In short: the map works without an account. Your markers, trips and settings stay in your browser unless you sign in or turn on a feature that asks a service for information. Signing in is optional; it adds a cloud copy and social features that you control. Google sign-in only confirms who you are. We do not sell your data, and the app contains no advertising or analytics code.

UAV Companion is an open-source map app for drone pilots, published at uavcompanion.com and operated by muffinMonday. It shows suitable-flight zones for most mainland Chinese provinces and no-fly zones for Singapore, and lets you check a spot, save places and trips, and (if you choose) sign in to sync them. The app is reference information, not flight advice.

1. Data that stays in your browser

Without an account, the following is stored only in your browser's local storage, on the device you use: saved places (markers), trips and their notes, map and display settings, privacy choices, a cache of weather, elevation and road routes, and a local diagnostics log. This data is not uploaded automatically. Browsers can clear it, and private browsing may lose it, so you can export a backup from the app at any time.

Your position is read by your browser only when you use a location feature. The launch-time centring uses your position on the device and is not sent anywhere. Check-in tracking and live location sharing are off until you turn them on.

3. Optional account

You can sign in with an email link or with Google. The account is held by Supabase, which the app talks to directly. Signing in is never required for the map, the zone check, search or local places and trips.

  • Sign-in record: the email address used for the sign-in link or your Google account, and the sign-in provider.
  • Cloud copy: one copy of your markers and trips, plus some settings. Check-in records are included only if you turn that on. Privacy choices and weather caches are not included. Up to 20 earlier versions are kept for 30 days so you can restore them.
  • Profile: a display name you type, an optional preset avatar, and optionally a short bio, a location you type, and interests. A friend code, whether you accept friend requests, whether your online status is shown, and the time you were last seen. The app does not show your email address to others.
  • Friends and blocks: friend requests and friendships, and the people you block. People add friends only with a friend code; there is no search of users.
  • Shared trips: the trip, its markers and notes, and the member list. Everyone who joins a shared trip can see and edit its contents, and sees the display names of its members and of whoever last changed it. The owner can delete the trip. Home markers are never shared.
  • Live location and online status: off by default. When you turn on live location for a trip, your latest position is sent to that trip's members and replaced with each update. A position older than about 2 minutes is no longer shown, and it is deleted after about 10 minutes. Automatic location sharing with friends works the same way and is off until you choose it. Online status sends a heartbeat every 30 seconds while you are signed in and the page is visible, unless you hide it.
  • Flight sessions: sessions you create or join, with the details you enter, your display name, your RSVP and attendance. A public session is visible to everyone using the app and shows its exact meeting coordinates, so only publish a place you are happy to share.
  • Progression: experience points, rank, achievements and counts of attendance, shown to you, to your friends and to members of trips you join, if you leave your profile visible.
  • Steam (optional): you can link a Steam account to earn experience points from playtime in a short list of approved simulator games. Linking signs you in with Steam's own sign-in page, and the app stores your 17-digit Steam ID. The app then asks Steam for your total playtime in those approved games only, and stores each game's last total and the minutes counted from it. Nothing else from your Steam account is read. The app never sends your playtime anywhere else.
  • Administrative record: a short record of certain account events, such as attendance, settings and deletions. It holds no free text. References to a deleted person are cleared when the account is deleted.

4. Google sign-in

When you choose Google, you are sent to Google, which asks you to approve sharing the basic identity information the app requests: your Google account identifier, your email address and your basic profile (openid, email and profile scopes). The app does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, contacts or any other Google service, and does not import anything from them.

The app uses this information only to sign you in and to link you to your account. Supabase keeps the account record for your sign-in, which can include the email address and the basic profile fields Google returns, such as a name and picture. The app does not display or use those Google name or picture fields. Google's own privacy policy applies to what Google does with your sign-in.

5. Sharing and sale

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising. The app contains no analytics or tracking code. Data leaves your browser only as described in sections 2 and 3, and otherwise only when you share it yourself: a backup or share link you create, a trip you share with its members, a friend connection, or a public flight session.

6. How long data is kept

  • Browser data stays until you delete it, clear site data or export and remove it.
  • Cloud copy versions: up to 20, kept for 30 days. Shared trip versions: up to 20, kept for 30 days.
  • Live positions: see section 3. They end a day after the trip.
  • Road routes for a shared trip are kept until the markers on that leg change or the trip is deleted.
  • Your profile, friends, sessions and progression are kept until you delete your profile or account.
  • Steam: unlinking removes your Steam ID but keeps the playtime already counted, so it is not counted twice if you link again. Deleting your profile removes the Steam link and its stored totals.

7. Your choices and deletion

  • Turn off a feature: weather, elevation, search, live location, automatic sharing, check-in and online status can each be turned off in the Privacy settings.
  • Export: you can download a backup file of your markers and trips at any time.
  • Sign out: you can sign out. Signing out can also remove this device's markers and trips after they are synced to your cloud copy.
  • Unlink Steam: available in the Steam section of your account (the account button's popover). It removes your Steam ID; the playtime already counted stays.
  • Delete your cloud copy: removes the cloud copy and its earlier versions. Markers and trips on the device stay.
  • Delete your profile and friends: removes your profile, friendships, blocks, session RSVPs and your progression.
  • Delete your account: removes your profile, friends, cloud copy and its versions. You leave every shared trip: a trip you own that others have joined passes to the member who joined first, and a trip nobody else joined is deleted. Your sign-in record is then deleted. Markers and trips on this device are not removed.

To ask about your data or to request something the app cannot do for you, use the project's GitHub Issues page: github.com/muffinMonday/uav-companion/issues.
