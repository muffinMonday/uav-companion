            Distances

            Every marker shows its straight-line distance to your nearest Home. A waypoint also shows its nearest Home point and Hotel, only from its own trip and only those whose dates overlap its own (one with no dates counts for every date). If a waypoint is farther than the max range from every such Home point and Hotel, it is shown in red; your Home does not count toward the range. Units also set the map's scale bar. Elevation and height limits stay in metres.

            What leaves your browser

            • Map tiles: the basemap provider you pick (Esri, OpenStreetMap or OpenTopoMap) sees which area you view.
            • Place search and lookup: online search sends your query to Photon or Nominatim, only when you search. Name lookup on import does the same with the names in the import, only if you turn it on above.
            • Weather and elevation: Open-Meteo.com, only as set above.
            • Never sent: marker names and notes, your saved markers, the wind limit, and these settings. Your location is read by the browser only when you press the location button.

            Welcome to UAV Companion!

            Check where you can fly a small drone, and plan the places you want to fly on your trip.

            1. Check a spot. Press and hold on the map for 2 seconds (or right-click) to see whether that spot is in a suitable-flight zone.
            2. Plan your trip. Create a trip under Trips, then drop markers for hotels, takeoff spots and photo spots. Or paste an AI-made itinerary and add it all at once. Press and hold a saved place to move it to another day.
            3. See the conditions. Open a marker for its zone verdict, its distance to your hotel and Home, and, if you turn it on, weather and the chance of rain.
            4. Your data stays yours. Markers and trips are saved only in this browser. Weather, elevation and name lookups are off until you allow them (shield button). Export a backup now and then.
