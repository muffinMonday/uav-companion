## Latest: responsive dialogs for unfolded phones (2026-11-14; owner authorized commit/push/deploy:live for this UI work only)

On Android Chrome `100vh` is the toolbar-hidden height, so when the visible area is shorter than the dialog (landscape unfolded: 850x620 visible), Chromium centres it with negative margins: dialog top -24 px, close button -11..25 px (reproduced by substituting a 700 px `100vh`; Playwright cannot show the real toolbar). ## Latest: Trips day-stop touch taps fixed (2026-11-14; owner authorized commit/push/deploy:live for this fix only)
- Root cause (measured with Playwright touch emulation and `elementsFromPoint`): in `@media (pointer:coarse)`, `.side-chip::after{position:absolute;inset:-8px 0}` (touch-target growth, `47feef8`) had no positioned parent, because `.side-chip` was missing from the `:where(...){position:relative}` list. On the Trips day card the chip is static, so the pseudo-element sized itself to `aside#panel`: an invisible 316 x ~400 px overlay that scrolls with the content. Taps on the stop that owns the chip (and on other stops under the overlay at some scroll positions) hit the chip and toggled Side Quests instead of opening the marker. A fine pointer (mouse) never got the rule. Ruled out: pointer/touch handler conflicts, `pointercancel`, re-render (taps on ordinary stops, the check-in button and the chip worked on the old code).
- Second defect, same symptom class: `reorder-ui.js` left a 400 ms capture-phase click suppressor after a drag, so a tap straight after a drop was swallowed (tap at 8 ms: swallowed; at 460 ms: works). It is now removed by the click it exists for, the next `pointerdown`, or the timer.
- Changes: `src/styles.css` (`.side-chip` added to the `:where` list), `src/reorder-ui.js` (suppressor lifetime), new browser suite `tests/tripstouch.mjs` (50 checks, CDP touch events: name and number taps, consecutive taps, Side Quest stop, chip fold/unfold, Side Quest row, check-in, hold-and-drag, tap right after the drop, vertical scroll, day swipe, Enter/Space, hit test of every row; phone portrait 390x844 and landscape 844x390), registered in `tests/run.mjs`, listed in `MODULES.md`. Without the two fixes the suite fails 4 checks per orientation (hit tests, Side Quest stop, post-drop tap); with them all pass.
- Results: unit 53/53; full `npm test` all 112 suites passed (498 s); build passes; screenshots of the trip/panel/poi screens (83) byte-identical to unmodified HEAD (one HEAD capture failed to complete, so 83 of 84 compared). Not verified: a real phone (iOS Safari, Android Chrome). Observed, not changed: with the map already on a stop, the popup opens after the 700 ms fallback timer in `flyToItem`, which feels slow.
