## Baseline failures: 100 of 237 suites fail before and after, identically
Re-run at `--jobs 2` (30 suites): the same ones fail, so none of these are load flakiness.
- **Region data absent (about 70 suites).** `public/data/**` (generated by `npm run build:data`, untracked) is missing, as is the raw source under `data/regions`: 8 unit suites crash on ENOENT (`region-labels`, `country-groups`, `yunnan`, `my-enr51`, `my-atz`, `vn-enr51`, `vn-ad`) and about 60 browser suites end with the console error "[map] zone data did not load" (`console errors`) or time out at 30 s waiting for zones or country buttons (fly-in, search, dashboard, poi1, shell, side, picker, gestures, ...). Airspace interpretation is therefore NOT exercised here (see limits).
- **Supabase environment absent (20 suites).** `.env.development.local` missing: areas-explorer/recover/attributes/photo, sessions*, progression, steam, cloud, friends, party, live, google-signin, shell-nav-explore/profile, marker-host-explorer, session-arrival/preview, autoshare, responsive.
- **Frozen snapshot / known product-test drift (documented in `CURRENT_STATUS.md` before this work):** `html-components-unit` and `html-dom` (assembled index.html hash), `contrast-unit` (3 literal z-index / font-size findings), `maplaunch`, `prefs`, `marker-content` (window DOM snapshot).
- **Timing-sensitive:** `motion` (3 failing assertions in the first run, 2 in the next two; assertion set differs between runs).
- **Fixed in this work:** (1) `areas` crashed at assertion 108 on a stale locator (`dialog strong` matched two nodes after the photo section was added); now 138 pass, 0 fail, and the suite fails only on the data-absent console error. (2) Parallel dev-server port clashes (`shell-nav` 5192/5193 vs `combined-ui`, `motor-diagnostics-ui`; `shell-nav-explore` 5194 vs `motor-diagnostics-ui`; `shell-nav-profile` 5195 vs `fourway-ui`): a release-tier run lost `motor-diagnostics-ui` with "Port 5193 is already in use". (3) `my-enr51` browser suite existed but was in no list; it is registered (it skips when the data was never built).
- No failing or flaky test was skipped, removed or weakened.

## What was consolidated
Nothing was removed. A scan of 2906 assertion labels across the 237 suites found 7 labels that appear in more than one suite: five are the same safety wording checked per region (Malaysia, Vietnam: "outside every polygon: unknown, never permission"; restricted-with-conditions wording), one is a database-error pass-through in two UI layers, one a settings save in two panel layouts. Each runs against different data or code, so none is demonstrably redundant. Overlap candidates for an owner review, not changed: `marker-host`, `-s5`..`-s8`, `-explorer` (six suites, about 400 s), `trip` (136 s) vs `trips`, `fourway-ui` (176 s) vs `bench-lab` (121 s). Time was saved by tiering and scheduling, not by deleting coverage.

## Coverage equivalence
- **Full = `npm test` = every suite that was in `tests/run.mjs`, plus `my-enr51`.** Nothing is dropped from it.
- **Release** keeps every unit suite (RLS, authentication, privacy, account deletion, XP, shared data, airspace and motor/preflight logic) and 28 browser suites (one per behaviour area, reasons in `tests/tiers.mjs`). Left to full only: the long motor/preflight browser suites (`fourway-ui`, `bench-lab`, `combined-ui`) because their safety logic is covered by the `bench-*`, `fourway-*` and `preflight-*` unit suites that stay in release.
- **Standalone `node --test` suites** (`*.test.mjs`: airspace GEOS integration, pilot winding, China CLI, Sichuan release, tile precision) are in no tier and were not before; they need local data and tools and fail or skip here for that reason (tile-precision 1 of 2, china-airspace 42 of 43, sichuan-release, airspace-integration 3 of 17 pass).

## Limits
Airspace interpretation and everything needing a hosted Supabase project cannot be verified in this container; those results must come from a machine with `public/data` and `.env.development.local`. The fast tier is a development aid.
