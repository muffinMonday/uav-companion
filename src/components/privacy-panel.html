Privacy settings

Most helpful online features are on by default; each one sends only what its card says, and every switch here turns it off. Automatic location sharing and automatic attendance stay off until you agree. As a guest, your markers, trips, notes and settings are kept in this browser; map tiles and any feature you leave on still contact the services named below. Signing in adds a cloud copy and social features.

Distances

Every marker shows its straight-line distance to your nearest Home. A waypoint also shows its nearest Home point and Accommodation, only from its own trip and only those whose dates overlap its own (one with no dates counts for every date). If a waypoint is farther than the max range from every such Home point and Accommodation, it is shown in red; your Home does not count toward the range. Units also set the map's scale bar. Elevation and height limits stay in metres.

Diagnostics

A short log of problems (failed requests, errors) is kept in this browser only: at most 500 entries, 200 KB and 7 days. It holds no positions, marker names, notes, tokens or account details, and nothing is sent anywhere. Download it if someone is helping you with a problem.

What leaves your browser

  • Map tiles: the basemap you pick is loaded from OpenStreetMap, OpenStreetMap France (the Humanitarian and CyclOSM styles), OpenTopoMap or Go Tiles by WP Go Maps (standard OSM in dark mode), which see which area you view. Go Tiles receives your IP address and page origin; Go Tiles privacy policy.
  • Place search and lookup: online search sends your query to Photon or Nominatim, only when you search. Name lookup on import does the same with the names in the import (on by default; switch it off above).
  • Check-in: on by default; your position is read by the browser only while check-in is on and the panel shows markers. It is saved with the marker in this browser, included in an export, and left out of share links.
  • Weather and elevation: Open-Meteo.com. Weather uses rounded coordinates (about 1 km) unless you choose Precise; elevation uses the exact marker or point location. Both are on by default and can be turned off above.
  • Nearby sessions, live sharing and attendance: these go to UAV Companion's own server (Supabase): the rounded map centre for nearby public sessions (on by default when signed in; for a guest only if switched on), your position for live sharing (signed in, once you agree), and position samples for automatic attendance (signed in, once you and the server have agreed).
  • As a guest: markers, trips and settings stay in this browser; signing in adds a cloud copy.
  • Never sent: marker names and notes, your saved markers, the wind limit, and these settings. Your location is read by the browser when you press the location button and, unless you turn it off above, once when the app opens; it stays on this device.
