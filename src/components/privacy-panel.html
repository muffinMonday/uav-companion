Distances

Every marker shows its straight-line distance to your nearest Home. A waypoint also shows its nearest Home point and Accommodation, only from its own trip and only those whose dates overlap its own (one with no dates counts for every date). If a waypoint is farther than the max range from every such Home point and Accommodation, it is shown in red; your Home does not count toward the range. Units also set the map's scale bar. Elevation and height limits stay in metres.

Diagnostics

A short log of problems (failed requests, errors) is kept in this browser only: at most 500 entries, 200 KB and 7 days. It holds no positions, marker names, notes, tokens or account details, and nothing is sent anywhere. Download it if someone is helping you with a problem.

What leaves your browser

  • Map tiles: the basemap you pick is loaded from OpenStreetMap, OpenStreetMap France (the Humanitarian and CyclOSM styles) or OpenTopoMap, which see which area you view.
  • Place search and lookup: online search sends your query to Photon or Nominatim, only when you search. Name lookup on import does the same with the names in the import, only if you turn it on above.
  • Check-in: your position is read by the browser only while check-in is on and the panel shows markers. It is saved with the marker in this browser, included in an export, and left out of share links.
  • Weather and elevation: Open-Meteo.com, only as set above.
  • Never sent: marker names and notes, your saved markers, the wind limit, and these settings. Your location is read by the browser when you press the location button and, unless you turn it off above, once when the app opens; it stays on this device.
