# Vietnam prohibited, restricted and danger areas (vn-enr51)

- Source: Vietnam Air Traffic Management Corporation (VATM) AIM, AIP Viet Nam **ENR 5.1**, edition **2026-10-01-AIRAC** (`https://aim.vatm.vn/images/stories/vnaic.vn/SanPhamDichVu/AIPVietNam/AIP/2026-10-01-AIRAC/html/eAIP/VV-ENR-5.1-en-GB.html`). Page SHA-256 `c84ed668903903cead2bd0c2e2e651d64bbec92373ca999e5141a21ad893f4b8` (214,430 bytes, fetched 2026-11-24; the exact page is committed at `data/regions/vn/vn-enr51/source/VV-ENR-5.1-en-GB.html`). Only ENR 5.1: no ENR 2, ENR 5.2, NOTAMs, supplements, activation tracking or third-party maps.
- Extraction: `node scripts/airspace/vn-enr51.mjs [--fetch]` parses the published tabulation and writes `data/regions/vn/vn-enr51/layers/{prohibited,restricted,danger}.geojson` and `exceptions.json`. Geometry only where the text defines it: explicit DMS coordinate polygons and WGS84 (Vincenty) centre/radius circles, reusing the geodesy of `my-enr51.mjs`. No coastline, border or other third-party geometry; nothing inferred.
- Result (2026-11-26): **74 of 74 areas represented = 71 exact + 3 approximate** (see "Approximate layer"). The strict extractor still converts 71 and lists 3 as exceptions (each now marked `approximated`). **74 areas parsed = 71 converted + 3 unresolved by the strict extractor** (P 4/4, R 20/21, D 47/49). 32 circles and 39 coordinate polygons. No area was excluded as foreign-administered (the source gives no such distinction).
- Strict-extractor exceptions (approximated, see below): **VVR19 Bien Hoa** (`external-reference`: parallels of airways W1 and R468, a runway-relative line and a 75 km circle about the Tan Son Nhat ARP; needs ENR 3 route geometry, not reconstructed). **VVD43, VVD45** (`source-inconsistent`): a vertex a full degree from its neighbours (VVD43 `200333N`, VVD45 `1062002E`); likely typos for `210333N` / `1052002E` but not corrected. The extractor's plausibility screen (no edge over 60 km, no extent over 150 km) found them; re-check after the next AIRAC.
- Recovery pass 2026-11-24 (official VATM material only, none recovered): the ENR 5.1 text of VVD43/VVD45 is identical in the 2025-01-23, 2025-09-04, 2026-01-22, 2026-03-19, 2026-05-14 and 2026-10-01 AIRAC editions, and the official chart ENR 5.1-11 (`graphics/9130`, byte-identical in 2025-01-23 and 2026-10-01) draws the same far vertices (about 1 degree away), so it is not independent evidence for `210333N` / `1052002E`. VVR19: ENR 3.1 gives AC (An Loc NDB 105621N 1071118E) and TSH, but no point `BU`, and PNH (Phnom Penh) is in AIP Cambodia; the Bien Hoa runway line and the extents of the "parallels" are undefined. None could be recovered exactly; the approximate layer below was added on 2026-11-26.
- **Approximate layer (3):** `node scripts/airspace/vn-enr51-approx.mjs` (run after the extractor) writes `layers/approx-{restricted,danger}.geojson` and `approx-provenance.json`; the 71 exact layers are untouched. Classes `approx-restricted` (severity 5) and `approx-danger` (severity 6) are `approximate: true`, so the app draws them as stripes with the approximate outline and a point inside reads "Potential restricted/danger airspace — approximate boundary" with the notice "Boundary approximated from official AIP chart" (same mechanism as Malaysia; `approximateNotice` in `src/legends.js` is worded per authority). Features keep P/R/D class, vertical limits and remarks, plus `geometryConfidence`, `reconstruction`, `uncertaintyM`.
  - **VVR19** (`approximate`, ~2 km): rebuilt from the AIP definition. Northern edge: 75 km arc about the Tan Son Nhat ARP 104914N 1063939E (AD 2 VVTS). Western: line 15 km north of R468 (TSH 104906N 1063902E to SAPEN 110112N 1061100E, ENR 3.1). Eastern: line 15 km west of W1 segment AC-BU, with BU read as BMT (Buon Ma Thuot; W1 runs BMT-ENRIN-AC on one 028 degree line, and the chart edge is parallel to it, 15 km west of AC). Southern: 5 km south of RWY 27L/09R at Bien Hoa; the runway coordinates are not in the eAIP, so the latitude (10.9296 N) is read from the official chart ENR 5.1-13 (`graphics/9132`, vector PDF georeferenced on six converted circle centres, residual at most 0.6 pt, about 0.5 km). Result 5083 km2; the chart outline lies within 1.2 km of it.
  - **VVD43** (`derived`): published polygon, vertex 4 `200333N 1052801E` interpreted as `210333N 1052801E` (a full degree south of the other three vertices; the corrected quadrilateral is about 30 km2). Chart ENR 5.1-11 reproduces the published vertex, so it is not independent evidence.
  - **VVD45** (`approximate`, up to ~10 km at one vertex): vertex 1 `210159N 1062002E` interpreted as `210159N 1052002E` (a degree east of the other three vertices; the intended longitude cannot be confirmed, this is the single-digit correction). About 79 km2.
  - Published and interpreted values are both kept in `approx-provenance.json` (`evidence`); the source text is not edited. Re-check against the next AIRAC.
- Validation per ring: closure, counter-clockwise winding, non-zero area, no self-intersection, coordinate window (lon 100-118, lat 6-24), circle radius within 0.5%, duplicate IDs (none). Vertical limits and remarks are kept verbatim (`UNL`, `M`, `AGL`, `SFC`, `To be specified`, `Unspecified` are not converted or invented).
- Semantics as Malaysia: `restrict`, classes **P** prohibited (not permitted), **R** restricted (conditions apply, never shown as a ban), **D** danger (caution). `partialCoverage: true`: outside the polygons the result is "no data", never "clear". `maxZoom 12`, archive 0.3 MB.
- Official charts: not used for the 71 exact areas (the text defines them); ENR 5.1-13 is used only for the VVR19 southern line and as a deviation check (above).
