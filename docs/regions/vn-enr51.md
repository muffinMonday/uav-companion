# Vietnam prohibited, restricted and danger areas (vn-enr51)

- Source: Vietnam Air Traffic Management Corporation (VATM) AIM, AIP Viet Nam **ENR 5.1**, edition **2026-10-01-AIRAC** (`https://aim.vatm.vn/images/stories/vnaic.vn/SanPhamDichVu/AIPVietNam/AIP/2026-10-01-AIRAC/html/eAIP/VV-ENR-5.1-en-GB.html`). Page SHA-256 `c84ed668903903cead2bd0c2e2e651d64bbec92373ca999e5141a21ad893f4b8` (214,430 bytes, fetched 2026-11-24; the exact page is committed at `data/regions/vn/vn-enr51/source/VV-ENR-5.1-en-GB.html`). Only ENR 5.1: no ENR 2, ENR 5.2, NOTAMs, supplements, activation tracking or third-party maps.
- Extraction: `node scripts/airspace/vn-enr51.mjs [--fetch]` parses the published tabulation and writes `data/regions/vn/vn-enr51/layers/{prohibited,restricted,danger}.geojson` and `exceptions.json`. Geometry only where the text defines it: explicit DMS coordinate polygons and WGS84 (Vincenty) centre/radius circles, reusing the geodesy of `my-enr51.mjs`. No coastline, border or other third-party geometry; nothing inferred.
- Result: **74 areas parsed = 71 converted + 3 unresolved** (P 4/4, R 20/21, D 47/49). 32 circles and 39 coordinate polygons. No area was excluded as foreign-administered (the source gives no such distinction).
- Unresolved: **VVR19 Bien Hoa** (`external-reference`: parallels of airways W1 and R468, a runway-relative line and a 75 km circle about the Tan Son Nhat ARP; needs ENR 3 route geometry, not reconstructed). **VVD43, VVD45** (`source-inconsistent`): a vertex a full degree from its neighbours (VVD43 `200333N`, VVD45 `1062002E`); likely typos for `210333N` / `1052002E` but not corrected. The extractor's plausibility screen (no edge over 60 km, no extent over 150 km) found them; re-check after the next AIRAC.
- Recovery pass 2026-11-24 (official VATM material only, none recovered): the ENR 5.1 text of VVD43/VVD45 is identical in the 2025-01-23, 2025-09-04, 2026-01-22, 2026-03-19, 2026-05-14 and 2026-10-01 AIRAC editions, and the official chart ENR 5.1-11 (`graphics/9130`, byte-identical in 2025-01-23 and 2026-10-01) draws the same far vertices (about 1 degree away), so it is not independent evidence for `210333N` / `1052002E`. VVR19: ENR 3.1 gives AC (An Loc NDB 105621N 1071118E) and TSH, but no point `BU`, and PNH (Phnom Penh) is in AIP Cambodia; the Bien Hoa runway line and the extents of the "parallels" are undefined. All three stay unresolved.
- Validation per ring: closure, counter-clockwise winding, non-zero area, no self-intersection, coordinate window (lon 100-118, lat 6-24), circle radius within 0.5%, duplicate IDs (none). Vertical limits and remarks are kept verbatim (`UNL`, `M`, `AGL`, `SFC`, `To be specified`, `Unspecified` are not converted or invented).
- Semantics as Malaysia: `restrict`, classes **P** prohibited (not permitted), **R** restricted (conditions apply, never shown as a ban), **D** danger (caution). `partialCoverage: true`: outside the polygons the result is "no data", never "clear". `maxZoom 12`, archive 0.3 MB.
- Official charts (ENR 5.1-11, -13, -15) were not used: the text defines every converted geometry.
- Licence: unknown. No reuse terms were checked on the eAIP; published on the owner's authorization of 2026-11-24 as planning reference. Remove or set `publish: false` if VATM objects.
