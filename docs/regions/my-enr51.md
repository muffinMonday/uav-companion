# Malaysia prohibited, restricted and danger areas (my-enr51)

- Source: Civil Aviation Authority of Malaysia, AIP Malaysia **ENR 5.1**, edition **AIP AMDT 04/26, effective 2026-11-03** (`https://aip.caam.gov.my/aip/eAIP/2026-11-03/html/eAIP/WM-ENR-5.1-en-MS.html`). Page SHA-256 `aba82af4d88b096cb0aa81767fb0212f1d856eea9e784343fd68974dda5d840a`. Only ENR 5.1: no ENR 2, NOTAMs, supplements, temporary areas, activation tracking or third-party maps.
- Extraction: `node scripts/airspace/my-enr51.mjs [--fetch] [--fetch-refs]` parses the published tabulation and writes `data/regions/my/my-enr51/layers/{prohibited,restricted,danger}.geojson` and `exceptions.json` (committed). Geometry only where the text defines it: coordinate polygons, geodesic circles (WGS84 Vincenty), latitude/longitude boxes, and arcs with a stated centre and radius. Arc centres named as a navaid, waypoint or ARP (VKB, VKG, Labuan DVOR/DME, Labuan ARP, ADLEX) take the coordinates CAAM publishes elsewhere (ENR 4.1, ENR 4.4, AD 2 WBKL), stored in `references.json` (`--fetch-refs` refreshes it). An arc whose direction is not published takes the shorter arc (at most 170 degrees; about 180 degrees stays unresolved) and the feature records that in `geometryNote`; endpoints must lie within 3% of the stated radius. A self-intersecting ring whose last vertex restates the first within 300 m is closed at the first vertex (`geometryNote`; only when that removes the self-intersection); a three-point path ending "to start" is a triangle. Anything else is excluded with a reason, never guessed. Rings are checked for closure, winding, area, self-intersection and the Malaysian coordinate window.
- Result (second pass, 2026-11-24): **108 numbered ENR 5.1 areas parsed = 86 Malaysian-administered + 8 Singapore + 14 Brunei**. Of the 86 Malaysian areas, **57 converted, 29 unresolved** (P 3 of 4, R 39 of 63, D 15 of 19). Baseline before this pass: 61 of 108 (42 of them Malaysian, all byte-identical now; `tests/fixtures/my-enr51-baseline-geometry.json`). Four unnumbered link/air corridors are skipped (`notAreas`).
- Recovered in the second pass (15): WMR105, WMR106, WMR108, WMR225A, WMR225B (VKB and Kluang arcs), WMR302A ("to start"), WMR418 and WBR601 (last vertex restated the start), WBR508-WBR511 (VSN arcs), WBR518 (Labuan ARP/DVOR and ADLEX), WBR603, WBR604 (VKG arcs).
- Excluded as not Malaysian-administered (kept in `exceptions.json` under `excluded`, with the evidence): **Singapore (8)** WSR2, WSD11, WSD13, WSD14, WSD15, WSD20, WSD44, WSD45 (WS-series; Singapore has its own dataset). **Brunei (14)** WBP503, WBP504, WBP506, WBR560/520, WBD507, WBD508, WBD509, WBD509A, WBD510, WBD511, WBD512, WBD513, WBD514, WBD515: explicit Brunei controlling authority (DCA Brunei, Brunei Shell Petroleum, HM Sultan's Flight), a named Brunei site (Jerudong, Berakas, Tutong, Binturan, Bukit Agok, Lumut Range), or, for WBD508, WBD514 and WBD515, only the published coordinates (Temburong; Tutong/Belait). The WB prefix alone is not used: WBR5xx/WBR6xx/WBD505 (Sabah, Sarawak) stay Malaysian.
- Unresolved Malaysian (29), reasons in `exceptions.json`: P WMP228; R WMR102B, WMR103B, WMR107, WMR225, WMR226, WMR239A, WMR239B, WMR240A, WMR240B, WMR403-WMR408, WMR416A-C, WMR419, WBR519, WBR520, WBR523, WBR524, WBR525; D WMD412A, WMD419, WMD420, WMD421. Causes: coastline, border, FIR, CTR/TMA or other airspace-boundary references (not reconstructed: would need uncertain interpretation or an external GIS dataset); WMR225/WMR226 (arc coordinates inconsistent within the entry, no stated closure); WBR523 (arc endpoints not on the stated radius); WMD421 (three points, no closing statement).
- Semantics: `restrict` with three classes that keep ICAO meaning: **P** prohibited (not permitted), **R** restricted (conditions apply: shown as restricted, never as a ban), **D** danger (caution, not a ban). Each polygon carries the published upper and lower limits, restriction type and remarks as static text; activation times are not evaluated. `partialCoverage: true` (coverage is measured against the 86 Malaysian-administered areas): outside the polygons the result is "no data", never "clear".
- Archive: `maxZoom 12` (sparse, large-area data; deeper zooms overzoom). The areas near Singapore make the archive load with Singapore's on a first visit (0.6 MB).
