# Malaysia prohibited, restricted and danger areas (my-enr51)

- Source: Civil Aviation Authority of Malaysia, AIP Malaysia **ENR 5.1**, edition **AIP AMDT 04/26, effective 2026-11-03** (`https://aip.caam.gov.my/aip/eAIP/2026-11-03/html/eAIP/WM-ENR-5.1-en-MS.html`). Page SHA-256 `aba82af4d88b096cb0aa81767fb0212f1d856eea9e784343fd68974dda5d840a`. Only ENR 5.1: no ENR 2, NOTAMs, supplements, temporary areas, activation tracking or third-party maps.
- Extraction: `node scripts/airspace/my-enr51.mjs [--fetch] [--fetch-refs]` parses the published tabulation and writes `data/regions/my/my-enr51/layers/{prohibited,restricted,danger}.geojson` and `exceptions.json` (committed). Geometry only where the text defines it: coordinate polygons, geodesic circles (WGS84 Vincenty), latitude/longitude boxes, and arcs with a stated centre and radius. Arc centres named as a navaid, waypoint or ARP (VKB, VKG, Labuan DVOR/DME, Labuan ARP, ADLEX) take the coordinates CAAM publishes elsewhere (ENR 4.1, ENR 4.4, AD 2 WBKL), stored in `references.json` (`--fetch-refs` refreshes it). An arc whose direction is not published takes the shorter arc (at most 170 degrees; about 180 degrees stays unresolved) and the feature records that in `geometryNote`; endpoints must lie within 3% of the stated radius. A self-intersecting ring whose last vertex restates the first within 300 m is closed at the first vertex (`geometryNote`; only when that removes the self-intersection); a three-point path ending "to start" is a triangle. Anything else is excluded with a reason, never guessed. Rings are checked for closure, winding, area, self-intersection and the Malaysian coordinate window.
- Result (third pass, 2026-11-24): **108 numbered ENR 5.1 areas parsed = 86 Malaysian-administered + 8 Singapore + 14 Brunei**. Of the 86 Malaysian areas, **64 are verified, 12 approximate (see below), 10 unresolved** (P 3 of 4, R 44 of 63, D 17 of 19). Progress: 42 Malaysian at the first pass, 57 after the second, 64 now; all 42 first-pass geometries stay byte-identical (`tests/fixtures/my-enr51-baseline-geometry.json`). Four unnumbered link/air corridors are skipped (`notAreas`).
- Recovered in the second pass (15): WMR105, WMR106, WMR108, WMR225A, WMR225B (VKB and Kluang arcs), WMR302A ("to start"), WMR418 and WBR601 (last vertex restated the start), WBR508-WBR511 (VSN arcs), WBR518 (Labuan ARP/DVOR and ADLEX), WBR603, WBR604 (VKG arcs).
- Recovered in the third pass (7), using other official CAAM definitions only: WMR102B (minus the Kerteh CTR, AD 2 WMKE 2.17), WMR103B (Kuantan CTR circle, AD 2 WMKD), WMR107 (Kuala Terengganu CTR, AD 2 WMKN), WMD412A and WMD420 (Butterworth TMA arc, ENR 2.1), WBR519 and WBR520 (Kota Kinabalu FIR western boundary, ENR 2.1, plus the Labuan ARP arc). `references.json` `boundaries` holds each transcription with its quoted source text. "Along <boundary>" between two published vertices becomes the boundary's own vertices (both vertices within 2 km of it, 5 km for the FIR line; the clearly shorter way round a closed boundary, otherwise unresolved). WBR519/WBR520 follow the FIR leg as the chord between the stated points. A polygon "excluding" a CTR is cut with a small polygon-difference routine (checked against a point-sampling test).
- Excluded as not Malaysian-administered (kept in `exceptions.json` under `excluded`, with the evidence): **Singapore (8)** WSR2, WSD11, WSD13, WSD14, WSD15, WSD20, WSD44, WSD45 (WS-series; Singapore has its own dataset). **Brunei (14)** WBP503, WBP504, WBP506, WBR560/520, WBD507, WBD508, WBD509, WBD509A, WBD510, WBD511, WBD512, WBD513, WBD514, WBD515: explicit Brunei controlling authority (DCA Brunei, Brunei Shell Petroleum, HM Sultan's Flight), a named Brunei site (Jerudong, Berakas, Tutong, Binturan, Bukit Agok, Lumut Range), or, for WBD508, WBD514 and WBD515, only the published coordinates (Temburong; Tutong/Belait). The WB prefix alone is not used: WBR5xx/WBR6xx/WBD505 (Sabah, Sarawak) stay Malaysian.
- Unresolved Malaysian (10) after the approximation pass, `category` in `exceptions.json`. **External-reference, not reconstructed (6):** WMR403 (Alor Setar/Butterworth control-zone boundaries not published as coordinates), WMR404 (railway, canal, runway arc), WMR405 and WMR406 (the permanent border danger area is not defined in ENR 5.1), WMR408 (public roads), WBR525 (the closing line at 1163200E does not meet the 20 NM arc end at 1162159E). **Ambiguous (4), never reconstructed:** WMR225, WMR226, WBR523, WMD421 (inconsistent or incomplete CAAM text, see `exceptions.json`).
- **Approximate layer (12), not official CAAM polygons:** `node scripts/airspace/my-enr51-approx.mjs` (run after the extractor) writes `layers/approx-{prohibited,restricted,danger}.geojson` and `approx-provenance.json`. Areas: WMR239A, WMR239B, WMR240A, WMR240B, WMR407, WMR416A, WMR416B, WMR416C, WMR419, WBR524, WMD419 and WMP228 (a 2 NM circle limited by the coast). CAAM supplies identity, class, anchor coordinates (kept exactly in every polygon), stated arcs, vertical limits and remarks; only the shape of the referenced **coastline or Malaysia/Thailand border** comes from **OpenStreetMap** (ODbL, extract `approx-sources/osm-extract.json`, refreshed with `scripts/airspace/my-enr51-osm.mjs`, simplified to 3 m). Each coast/border edge is pushed outward from the area by a buffer of 500 to 2500 m (1.25 x the largest distance between a CAAM coast/border anchor and the OSM line), tapering to zero at the anchors, so the polygon over-covers rather than under-covers. Where a boundary follows a verified area (WMR416 for WMR416A to C), that exact CAAM geometry is used. Each polygon was overlaid on the official CAAM chart and matches its hatched area. The implied bearing-270 line in WMR416C and the typo "005220E" (read 1005220E) in WMR407 are recorded in the provenance `notes`. Provenance (`approx-provenance.json`): CAAM ID, class, edition, anchors, referenced feature type, secondary dataset and licence, method, buffer, area, notes, limitations.
- **Verified vs approximate in the app:** the layers are separate classes (`approx-*`, `approximate: true`, severity 4 to 6 so verified zones always sort and decide first). They are drawn as stripes in the P/R/D colour with a purple outline (`--zone-approx-line`, lighter in the dark theme; never green) under the verified fills. A point inside one reads "Potential restricted airspace — approximate boundary" (prohibited, danger likewise) with the reconstruction warning, never an exact CAAM verdict; where a verified zone also holds the point, the verified zone decides. Outside every polygon stays unknown.
- **Counts:** 86 Malaysian = **64 verified + 12 approximate + 10 unresolved**; verified P 3/4, R 44/63, D 17/19. 108 parsed = 86 + 8 Singapore + 14 Brunei (unchanged exclusions).
- **Inferred arcs (checked 2026-11-24):** the 13 areas whose arc direction CAAM does not state (WMR105, WMR106, WMR107, WMR108, WMR225A, WMR225B, WBR508 to WBR511, WBR518 to WBR520) were overlaid on the official CAAM charts (ENR 6-7 Kuala Lumpur, AMDT 03/2024, `graphics/265779.pdf`; ENR 6-9 Sabah/Sarawak, AMDT 03/25, `graphics/288679.pdf`; vector PDFs georeferenced from their graticule ticks). All 13 shorter-arc readings match the depicted areas (WMR225B is the least clear on the chart); none was corrected or removed. `ARC_VERIFIED` in `scripts/airspace/my-enr51.mjs` records this and the extractor emits an inferred arc only for IDs listed there; the feature carries `arcVerifiedAgainst`. The charts are older editions than the 2026-11-03 table.
- Semantics: `restrict` with three classes that keep ICAO meaning: **P** prohibited (not permitted), **R** restricted (conditions apply: shown as restricted, never as a ban), **D** danger (caution, not a ban). Each polygon carries the published upper and lower limits, restriction type and remarks as static text; activation times are not evaluated. `partialCoverage: true` (coverage is measured against the 86 Malaysian-administered areas): outside the polygons the result is "no data", never "clear".
- Archive: `maxZoom 12` (sparse, large-area data; deeper zooms overzoom). The areas near Singapore make the archive load with Singapore's on a first visit (0.6 MB).

- Not verified: boundary fidelity of the verified geometry against CAAM GIS data (none is published); the verified/approximate split is described above.
