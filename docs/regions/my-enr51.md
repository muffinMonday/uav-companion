# Malaysia prohibited, restricted and danger areas (my-enr51)

- Source: Civil Aviation Authority of Malaysia, AIP Malaysia **ENR 5.1**, edition **AIP AMDT 04/26, effective 2026-11-03** (`https://aip.caam.gov.my/aip/eAIP/2026-11-03/html/eAIP/WM-ENR-5.1-en-MS.html`). Page SHA-256 `aba82af4d88b096cb0aa81767fb0212f1d856eea9e784343fd68974dda5d840a`. Only ENR 5.1: no ENR 2, NOTAMs, supplements, temporary areas, activation tracking or third-party maps.
- Extraction: `node scripts/airspace/my-enr51.mjs [--fetch]` parses the published tabulation and writes `data/regions/my/my-enr51/layers/{prohibited,restricted,danger}.geojson` and `exceptions.json` (committed). Geometry only where the text defines it exactly: coordinate polygons, geodesic circles (WGS84 Vincenty), latitude/longitude boxes, and arcs with a stated centre, radius and direction. Anything else is excluded with a reason, never guessed. Rings are checked for closure, winding, area, self-intersection, the Malaysian coordinate window and (circles) radius within 0.5%.
- Result: **108 numbered areas, 61 converted, 47 unresolved** (P 5 of 7, R 25 of 65, D 31 of 36). Four unnumbered link/air corridors (not designated areas) are skipped and listed under `notAreas`.
- Unresolved, with reasons in `exceptions.json`: P WMP228, WBP503; R WMR102B, WMR103B, WMR105, WMR106, WMR107, WMR108, WMR225, WMR225A, WMR225B, WMR226, WMR239A, WMR239B, WMR240A, WMR240B, WMR302A, WMR403, WMR404, WMR405, WMR406, WMR407, WMR408, WMR416A, WMR416B, WMR416C, WMR418, WMR419, WSR2, WBR508, WBR509, WBR510, WBR511, WBR518, WBR519, WBR520, WBR523, WBR524, WBR525, WBR601, WBR603, WBR604; D WMD412A, WMD419, WMD420, WMD421, WBD513. Main causes: coastline, border, CTR/TMA or other airspace-boundary references; arcs centred on a navaid with no coordinates or without a direction; three self-intersecting or open source rings.
- Semantics: `restrict` with three classes that keep ICAO meaning: **P** prohibited (not permitted), **R** restricted (conditions apply: shown as restricted, never as a ban), **D** danger (caution, not a ban). Each polygon carries the published upper and lower limits, restriction type and remarks as static text; activation times are not evaluated. `partialCoverage: true`: outside the polygons the result is "no data", never "clear".
- Archive: `maxZoom 12` (sparse, large-area data; deeper zooms overzoom). The areas near Singapore make the archive load with Singapore's on a first visit (0.6 MB).
- Licence: unknown. No copyright or reuse terms were found on the eAIP (GEN 0.1, edition index); published on the owner's authorization of 2026-11-24 as planning reference. Remove or set `publish: false` if CAAM objects.
- Not verified: boundary fidelity against CAAM's charts; the 4 WS* (Singapore FIR) areas overlap Singapore's own data and are shown as published in ENR 5.1.
